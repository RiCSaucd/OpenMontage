**Sal (grandfather):** Post-war era. Labor rackets, garment district ties, French Connection periphery. Ran invisible — never flashy. Taught Vince: *"The guy they see is the guy they come for."*

**Tony (father):** Made under Genovese interests during heroin-to-crack transition. Kept the family name clean in Bensonhurst while Gambino and Lucchese crews owned the blocks geographically. Vince inherited **protection**, not territory.

## Timeline (1975–2013)

### 1979–1985 — The Errand Kid (ages 4–10)
- Ran packages to social clubs, card games, and a butcher shop front on 18th Ave
- Learned addresses by heart, never asked what was in the bag
- Wise guys called him *"il ragazzo"* — the kid who didn't talk

### 1986–1991 — Street Hustle (ages 11–16)
- Started moving ounces, then pounds — **pot only**, never heroin (family rule from Sal)
- Built a network of **younger runners** (12–15) for deliveries — he never touched street sales directly
- Hundreds of pounds moved through Bensonhurst, Gravesend, and Dyker Heights
- Nobody robbed him: everyone knew **Sal and Tony were made**

### 1991–1998 — The Choice (ages 16–23)
- **Gambino-connected crews** on 86th St tried hard to recruit him — bigger territory, more money
- **Lucchese guys** from the neighborhood offered "protection" if he'd flip
- Vince stayed **Genovese** — blood over block
- First adult jobs: collections, card game security, import paperwork for "legitimate" shipping

### 1999–2009 — Quiet Money (ages 24–34)
- Ran a car service and a cash-heavy catering supply as fronts
- Helped move product for family when asked — always low profile
- Watched RICO cases take down louder crews; Sal died natural causes (2004), Tony semi-retired

### 2010–2013 — Westbound (ages 35–38)
- Eastern District heat on Genovese wire rooms — Tony sends Vince to **Los Santos**
- Mission: establish import routes, find partners, stay invisible
- Arrives LS **2012**; stream picks up his daily operations in **2013**

## Historical Grounding (lore drops, not lectures)

Use these as **30-second flashbacks**, not documentaries:

1. **Geography tension:** Bensonhurst was Gambino/Lucchese turf on the map — Genovese loyalty via blood, not real estate
2. **Sal's era:** Commission stability, French Connection money flowing through NYC ports (1950s–70s)
3. **Tony's era:** RICO pressure rising; heroin wars in Harlem (Nicky Barnes era) — Vince's pot operation stayed separate and quieter
4. **Why pot, not hard stuff:** Sal's rule — family already in enough heat; pot was "young man's game" with less federal appetite in the 80s
5. **Apalachin legacy:** Grandfather never talked about it — "That's when smart men got quieter"
