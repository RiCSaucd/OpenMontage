---

## 1. Core Identity

### Mission
Deliver immersive **GTA mafia roleplay** rooted in authentic Brooklyn and Genovese family lore — live on Twitch and Kick, clipped for TikTok and YouTube. Entertainment first; history flavor second.

### Vision
Build a loyal RP audience that follows Vince's arc from Bensonhurst street kid to Los Santos operator — with highlight clips that travel and a community that knows the difference between **fiction/RP** and real-world crime.

### Unique Value
- **Persistent character** with deep, playable backstory (not random weekly personas)
- **Real historical texture** without lecture mode (optional Nexus cross-audience)
- **Multi-platform native:** live on Twitch/Kick, VOD + Shorts on YouTube, viral hooks on TikTok
- **Creator discipline:** same operational rigor as Nexus AI Media, different tone

### Brand Personality
**The Quiet Operator with Brooklyn Steel**

- Calm, confident, in-character intensity
- Streetwise humor, not cartoon gangster
- Loyalty and family code as drama engine
- Never aspirational "crime lifestyle" marketing

**Tone keywords:** Brooklyn noir, loyalty, restraint, neighborhood pride, fish-out-of-water in LS

**Avoid:** Glorifying real violence, slurs, real family names of living mob figures, "how to" criminal instruction, edgy shock for clicks

### Taglines (test on platforms)
1. **"Same rules. Different city."** (primary)
2. "Theswampdon — Genovese blood, GTA streets"
3. "The kid who ran errands. The man who stayed quiet."
4. "Live mafia RP — Brooklyn roots, Los Santos heat"

### Stream disclaimer (panel + YouTube description)
> All characters and storylines are **fiction for roleplay entertainment** in Grand Theft Auto. Not affiliated with any real criminal organization. Not instructional. 18+ recommended for game content.

## 2. Audience Personas

### Primary: GTA RP Regulars
- **Age:** 21–40, male-skewing, US/EU
- **Wants:** Consistent character, serious RP, drama, heist/action, chat participation
- **Platforms:** Twitch, Kick live; catches clips on TikTok

### Secondary: Crime-Drama / Mafia Curious
- **Age:** 25–50, watches Sopranos-era content, true crime (including Nexus audience overlap)
- **Wants:** Authentic flavor, lore drops, "feels real" without being a documentary
- **Conversion:** Short lore clips → live streams

### Tertiary: Clip Scrollers
- **Age:** 18–35, TikTok/Shorts first
- **Wants:** 3-second hook, funny or intense moment, character voice
- **Conversion:** Follow for "full RP on Twitch"
