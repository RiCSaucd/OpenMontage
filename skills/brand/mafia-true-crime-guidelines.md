# Brand Skill — Mafia / Organized-Crime True-Crime Guidelines

## When to Use

Required alongside `brand/respected-historian-tone` for Nexus true-crime Shorts about Mafia, mob, or organized-crime history.

## Content boundaries

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Court records, trials, congressional hearings, reputable journalism | Invented dialogue presented as fact |
| Institutional context (unions, ports, politics, Prohibition economics) | How-to crime instruction |
| Consequences: prison, informant fallout, community harm | Celebrating hits, intimidation, or “made man” mythology as aspiration |
| Archival photos, newsreels, maps, documents | Graphic violence recreation for shock |
| Contested myths labeled as myth | Presenting rumor as settled history |

## Visual rules

- Prefer **archival footage and stills**, public-domain / licensed stock, maps, documents.
- AI video (Kling / Runway / Higgsfield) only for **atmospheric / period texture** when archival gaps exist — never for stylized murder set-pieces.
- No blood sprays, slow-mo weapon fetish, champagne-and-guns lifestyle looks.

## Script rules

- Open with a concrete historical hook (date, place, or documented event), not a gore tease.
- Keep runtime focused: one thesis, two supporting beats, one consequence / context landing.
- Attribute contested claims: “popular accounts say…; the record shows…”

## Music & audio

- Sparse, non-triumphant underscore.
- Leave clean VO layover windows; user records the final narration.

## Escalation

If the brief requires graphic violence recreation or glorification to “work,” stop and ask the user to reframe — do not produce it under this brand.
