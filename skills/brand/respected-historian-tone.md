# Brand Skill — Respected Historian Tone

## When to Use

Required for every `nexus-true-crime-short` production (and any Nexus Mafia / organized-crime Short). Apply at **research**, **proposal**, and **script** stages; re-check at edit and compose.

## Voice

Speak like a careful historian on camera — measured, sourced, and sober. Prefer:

- Documented chronology over lore
- Specific places, dates, and institutional context
- Human cost and community impact
- Plain diction; short sentences for Shorts

Avoid:

- Tabloid hype, cliffhanger clickbait that invents facts
- Glamorizing wealth, power, or “respect” culture
- Treating violence as spectacle or entertainment payoff
- First-person “I was there” inventiveness

## Non-negotiables

1. **Documented history first.** Separate record from myth in research; flag contested claims.
2. **Human cost required.** At least one beat must acknowledge victims, families, or civic harm.
3. **No lifestyle porn.** No hero-weapon framing, blood porn, or celebratory montage of crime.
4. **User records final VO.** Scripts supply timing cues and scratch reads only; do not treat TTS as the master track unless the user explicitly overrides.
5. **Sparse music.** Dark ambient / restrained underscore only — never triumphant or anthemic.

## Checkpoint note

Every research / proposal / script checkpoint should mention this skill was applied.
